Security Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03

We don't collect or store your personal data

Neogalleries is a fully client-side application. There is no server, no database and no account system. Everything runs in your own browser, on your own machine.

The following data is never collected, transmitted, stored or shared by Neogalleries:

  • Your email address
  • Your Neocities username and password
  • Your Neocities API key
  • Your name, location or any other personal information
  • Any analytics, tracking or telemetry data
  • Any cookies set by this panel

If you type your credentials or an API key into the settings form, that value only lives in your browser's local storage, on your device. It is sent exclusively to Neocities' own API, over HTTPS, so Neocities can authenticate you. Neogalleries has no access to it and can never read, log or transmit it.

Clearing your browser's site data removes everything Neogalleries has stored. Nothing about you survives on any Neogalleries system, because none exists.

We don't use your content

Any content you create, upload, import or write through Neogalleries — gallery entries, images, captions, descriptions, tags, notes or anything else — remains entirely yours.

We do not use it, and we never will. Specifically, we do not:

  • Use your content for training, analysis or any form of machine learning
  • Use your content to train, fine-tune or evaluate any model or AI system
  • Republish, redistribute, mirror, showcase or feature your content
  • Sell, license, rent or otherwise monetize your content
  • Use your content for advertising, profiling or marketing

Your images and texts are written only to the Neocities bucket that you configured. They are stored by Neocities under Neocities' own terms, and are governed by Neocities' privacy policy, not by this one.

Third parties

The panel is hosted as static files on GitHub Pages and loads its source from GitHub. These hosts may keep standard server logs. The panel has no trackers, no third-party scripts and no third-party embeds.

The only third party that ever receives anything is Neocities, and only the data required to perform the operation you asked for.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security issue, please report it privately through the project's GitHub repository rather than opening a public issue.
